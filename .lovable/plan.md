# Minimal, Quiet Interface

## Goal
Make the whole product feel simpler and calmer without removing race information or working features. Keep the dark identity and blue accent, but let content and actions—not decoration—set the hierarchy.

## Public pages
- Simplify the header to the brand, three primary destinations, language control, and one clear “Add race” action.
- Remove the announcement strip, decorative seals, corner frames, ornamental rules, numbered labels, technical metadata, and animated cursor effects.
- Keep the homepage photo and one primary calendar button, with shorter typography and a quieter image treatment.
- Replace large cinematic inner-page banners with compact image headers containing only the page title and, when useful, one short supporting line.
- Reduce the footer to brand, essential navigation, language control, and contact information.

## Calendar and race details
- Make the calendar controls a single compact toolbar: view, month/year, previous/next, and export.
- Remove the extra section heading and ornament above the calendar.
- Simplify calendar cells, selected states, and race markers while preserving keyboard navigation, focus return, and mobile behavior.
- Make list rows easier to scan by prioritizing date, race, location, distance, and the essential actions; move secondary facts into race details.
- Keep save, compare, registration, course map, and calendar export functions, but use compact icon-led controls.
- Simplify the date detail popup/bottom sheet and comparison table to one clear information hierarchy.

## Organizers, results, sign-in, and management
- Present organizers and results as clean bordered lists rather than framed decorative grids.
- Simplify sign-in and Add Race dialogs, shortening helper copy where it repeats labels or button intent.
- Apply the same restrained spacing, headings, controls, and borders to organizer and admin workspaces without changing permissions or workflows.

## Visual system
- Keep black, white/gray, and the existing blue as the only dominant palette.
- Use normal tracking, fewer all-caps labels, one clear heading scale, thin neutral dividers, and no shadows or glow effects.
- Use motion only for state changes and opening/closing views; respect reduced-motion preferences.
- Use the existing semantic color system throughout, including all button and form states.

## Validation
- Check every public page at desktop and phone widths for clipping, overlap, and readable hierarchy.
- Verify navigation, calendar month changes, date details, list view, save/compare, exports, Add Race, and sign-in still work.
- Confirm every content page retains its existing metadata and accessibility behavior.
